Privacy

Privacy policy

We follow the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173). This page explains what we collect, why, and how long we keep it.

What we collect

  • Your messages when you chat with our assistant on this website.
  • Your full name and mobile number, only when you give them to us, for example to book a site visit, reserve a house or talk to an agent.
  • The house you asked about and the date you would like to visit.

We never ask for payment, card or ID details on this website, and no payment is ever taken here.

Why we collect it

  • To answer your questions about our houses.
  • To contact you by call or text about the house, visit or reservation you asked about.

We don't sell your information.

The assistant

Our chat assistant is a computer program (AI), not a person. It answers from the information Grab A House gives it and can make mistakes, so an agent always confirms prices, availability and computations.

How long we keep it

We keep chats and requests for 12 months, then delete them or remove your name and number.
